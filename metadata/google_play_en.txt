Basir Accounting System - The Cognitive Financial Intelligence Platform

Basir is an accounting application designed for Saudi enterprises. It includes local e-invoicing capabilities that require approved ZATCA integration, clearance/reporting validation, and production verification before any compliance claim is made.

💎 Key Features:
- **Cognitive Hexagon Architecture**: Six specialized AI agents (Auditors, Tax Experts, Strategists) analyze every transaction in real-time.
- **E-invoicing capability**: Local cryptographic stamping and XML validation; ZATCA Phase 2 compliance remains subject to approved integration and production verification.
- **Multi-Currency Treasury**: Manage global transactions with our "Platinum Treasury" vault, featuring real-time liquidity forecasting.
- **Forensic Audit Engine**: Deep historical scrutiny and anomaly detection to safeguard your business against fraud.
- **Smart Reports**: One-click VAT returns, IFRS-compliant financial statements, and intelligent insights.
