## Introduction

This document defines the strategic requirements for the Basir Accounting System. The objective is to transform the strategic vision from a theoretical framework into an actionable, measurable, and trackable execution plan.

### Problem Statement

- Strategic visions often remain stagnant theoretical documents.
- Difficulty in translating high-level strategy into daily operational actions.
- Ambiguity in priorities and benchmarks.
- Friction in tracking progress toward long-term strategic goals.

### Solution

Translate every strategic pillar into clear, measurable requirements accompanied by specific user stories and acceptance criteria.

### Scope

- Market and Competitor Analysis.
- Business Model and Monetization Logic.
- Growth and Geographic Expansion Strategy.
- Product Roadmap and Feature Prioritization.
- Financial and Operational Metrics.
- Team and Resource Allocation.
- Risk Management Framework.

## Glossary

- **Strategic Vision**: The 5-year long-term vision for the project.
- **Product-Market Fit**: Alignment between the product functionality and market demands.
- **Freemium**: A business model offering both free and premium tiers.
- **North Star Metric**: The primary metric reflecting product value and success.
- **OKR (Objectives and Key Results)**: A goal-setting framework for defining and tracking outcomes.
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market)**: The total market demand for a product/service.
- **SAM (Serviceable Available Market)**: The portion of TAM within geographic reach.
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)**: The portion of SAM that can be realistically captured.
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**: Total cost to acquire a new customer.
- **LTV (Lifetime Value)**: Projected total revenue from a single customer relationship.
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**: Normalized monthly subscription revenue.
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**: Normalized annual subscription revenue.
- **Churn Rate**: The rate at which customers stop using the service.
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score)**: A metric measuring customer loyalty and satisfaction.
- **Moat**: A sustainable competitive advantage.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Market Analysis and Intelligence

**User Story:** As a strategy team, we need a deep understanding of the market and competitors to make data-driven decisions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN analyzing the market, the system SHALL document TAM, SAM, and SOM with specific verified figures.
2.  WHEN identifying competitors, the system SHALL analyze a minimum of 5 primary competitors, documenting strengths and weaknesses.
3.  WHEN identifying gaps, the system SHALL document at least 3 critical market voids.
4.  WHEN identifying opportunities, the system SHALL define at least 3 "Golden Opportunities" with estimated potential.
5.  WHEN reviewing market analysis, the system SHALL refresh the data quarterly.

### Requirement 2: Customer Segmentation

**User Story:** As a product and marketing team, we need to understand target customer segments to build and market the correct solution.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN defining a customer segment, the system SHALL document size, core needs, pain points, and willingness to pay.
2.  WHEN identifying the primary segment, the system SHALL designate one segment as the highest priority.
3.  WHEN building a persona, the system SHALL include demographics, psychographics, behaviors, goals, and pain points.
4.  WHEN targeting a segment, the system SHALL define a specific acquisition strategy for that segment.
5.  WHEN measuring success, the system SHALL track the penetration rate per segment.

### Requirement 3: Business Model and Monetization

**User Story:** As a founder and investor, we need a clear and sustainable business model to ensure project longevity.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN defining the business model, the system SHALL document the selected model (Freemium, Subscription, etc.) with technical rationale.
2.  WHEN establishing pricing, the system SHALL define tier pricing based on market research and value-based logic.
3.  WHEN forecasting revenue, the system SHALL build a financial model with transparent assumptions (conversion, churn).
4.  WHEN calculating Unit Economics, the system SHALL compute the LTV:CAC ratio and target > 3:1.
5.  WHEN reviewing the model, the system SHALL conduct sensitivity testing and identify the break-even point.

### Requirement 4: Competitive Differentiation (Moat)

**User Story:** As a product team, we need unique competitive features to differentiate the system and build a sustainable moat.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN defining a competitive advantage, the system SHALL document the feature, provided value, and mimicry resistance.
2.  WHEN building the moat, the system SHALL identify at least 3 sustainable competitive advantages.
3.  WHEN evaluating a feature, the system SHALL rate it on uniqueness, value, and defensibility.
4.  WHEN developing a feature, the team SHALL ensure alignment with the overarching strategic vision.
5.  WHEN measuring impact, the system SHALL track adoption rate and user satisfaction for every unique feature.

### Requirement 5: Growth Strategy and Execution

**User Story:** As a growth team, we need a clear growth strategy to achieve the defined project targets.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN planning growth, the system SHALL define at least 4 growth phases with clear objectives for each.
2.  WHEN identifying growth channels, the system SHALL select at least 5 channels with estimated CAC for each.
3.  WHEN executing a campaign, the system SHALL track performance against the defined KPIs.
4.  WHEN measuring growth, the system SHALL compute the monthly growth rate vs. targets.
5.  WHEN optimizing growth, the system SHALL identify bottlenecks and execute A/B testing.

### Requirement 6: Product Strategy and Roadmap

**User Story:** As a product team, we need a clear roadmap to define what to build and when.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN planning the product, the system SHALL define a minimum 12-month rolling roadmap.
2.  WHEN defining a feature, the team SHALL link it to a specific strategic objective.
3.  WHEN prioritizing, the team SHALL utilize a standardized framework (e.g., RICE or ICE).
4.  WHEN launching a feature, the team SHALL define success metrics prior to deployment.
5.  WHEN reviewing the roadmap, the team SHALL update it quarterly based on feedback.

### Requirement 7: Financial Planning and Controls

**User Story:** As a founder and investor, we need a clear financial plan to ensure sustained operation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN planning financially, the system SHALL maintain a minimum 3-year financial model.
2.  WHEN defining the budget, the system SHALL allocate resources across development, marketing, and operations.
3.  WHEN tracking expenses, the system SHALL monitor the monthly burn rate.
4.  WHEN calculating runway, the system SHALL compute the remaining months of operation.
5.  WHEN requiring funding, the system SHALL define the required capital and its planned utilization.

### Requirement 8: Team and Resource Management

**User Story:** As an operations manager, we need a clear plan for the team and resources to build the right organization.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN planning the team, the system SHALL define personnel requirements per quarter.
2.  WHEN hiring, the team SHALL define required roles, skills, and the recruitment timeline.
3.  WHEN establishing budget, the system SHALL calculate projected personnel costs.
4.  WHEN evaluating performance, the team SHALL define KPIs for every organizational role.
5.  WHEN planning growth, the team SHALL link organizational scaling to product growth.

### Requirement 9: Metrics and Key Results (OKRs)

**User Story:** As a team, we need clear metrics to know if we are succeeding.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN defining the North Star Metric, the system SHALL designate one primary metric reflecting delivered value.
2.  WHEN defining OKRs, the team SHALL define 3-5 objectives per quarter with 3-5 key results for each.
3.  WHEN tracking metrics, the system SHALL maintain a dashboard displaying all primary KPIs.
4.  WHEN reviewing performance, the team SHALL audit metrics weekly against targets.
5.  WHEN achieving a target, the team SHALL document the success and define the next horizon.

### Requirement 10: Risk Management

**User Story:** As a leadership team, we need to identify and manage risks to prevent strategic disruptions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN identifying risks, the system SHALL document at least 5 primary strategic risks.
2.  WHEN evaluating a risk, the system SHALL rate the likelihood and impact.
3.  WHEN planning mitigation, the team SHALL define at least 3 actions for every high-level risk.
4.  WHEN monitoring risks, the leadership SHALL review the risk register monthly.
5.  WHEN a risk materializes, the team SHALL execute the pre-defined contingency plan.

### Requirement 11: Regional Expansion Strategy (Phase 2+)

**User Story:** As a strategy team, we need a clear regional expansion plan to successfully enter new Arab markets.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN planning expansion, the system SHALL target at least 5 primary markets (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, Jordan, Kuwait).
2.  WHEN entering a new market, the system SHALL provide integration with local tax systems (UAE VAT, Egypt VAT, Jordan Sales Tax).
3.  WHEN serving a region, the system SHALL provide appropriate cultural localization (terminology, calendars, currency).
4.  WHEN evaluating a market, the system SHALL analyze size, competitors, and regulatory requirements.
5.  WHEN launching in a new country, the team SHALL identify a local partner or regulatory expert.

### Requirement 12: Multi-Currency and Localization

**User Story:** As a business user, we need multi-currency and multi-language support to serve clients in different Arab nations.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1.  WHEN selecting a currency, the system SHALL support at least 12 regional currencies (SAR, AED, EGP, JOD, KWD, etc.).
2.  WHEN requiring an exchange rate, the system SHALL provide live or daily historical rates.
3.  WHEN using the calendar, the system SHALL support multiple systems (Hijri, Gregorian, Coptic).
4.  WHEN displaying numerals, the system SHALL support both Western and Eastern Arabic numerals with smart switching.
5.  WHEN customizing an invoice, the system SHALL support multi-language templates (Arabic, English).

## Non-Functional Requirements

### Clarity and Communication

- All requirements must be transparent and understandable by all team members.
- Utilize precise, direct, and professional technical English.
- Avoid ambiguous terminology without explicit definition.

### Measurability

- All objectives must be quantifiable.
- Define clear metrics and unit measures for every goal.
- Set specific numerical targets for all KPIs.

### Actionability

- All requirements must be feasible and implementation-ready.
- Clearly define ownership and responsibilities.
- Establish definitive timelines for every requirement.

### Flexibility

- The strategy must remain agile and adaptable to market shifts.
- Conduct quarterly strategic reviews and adjustments.
- Build the capacity to pivot based on data-driven insights.

### Alignment

- Ensuring all requirements strictly align with the overarching long-term vision.
- Zero tolerance for contradictions between various requirements.
- Maintain clear and consistent hierarchical prioritization.
