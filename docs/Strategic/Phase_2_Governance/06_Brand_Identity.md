## Brand Overview

### Brand Name

**Basir** (بصير) - "The Insightful One" in Arabic

- Primary: "Financial Clarity for the Arab World"
- Secondary: "See Your Finances Clearly"

Transform complex financial data into clear, actionable insights through intelligent automation.

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice

- **Professional**: Expert but approachable
- **Trustworthy**: Reliable and secure
- **Clear**: Simple explanations
- **Helpful**: Solution-oriented

### Tone Guidelines

| Context       | Tone                         |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Success       | Encouraging, celebratory     |
| Error         | Empathetic, solution-focused |
| Onboarding    | Warm, guiding                |
| Documentation | Clear, professional          |

## Arabic Language Guidelines

### Terminology

Use Arabic financial terms where natural, with English in technical contexts.

| Concept  | Arabic  | Notes        |
| -------- | ------- | ------------ |
| Invoice  | فاتورة  | Not "إنفويس" |
| Customer | عميل    | Not "كاستمر" |
| Tax      | ضريبة   | Standard     |
| Budget   | ميزانية | Not "بادجت"  |

### RTL Design Rules

- All layouts RTL-first
- Icons mirror where appropriate
- Numbers: Western Arabic (1,2,3) default
- Dates: Both Hijri and Gregorian

## Asset Usage

### Photography Style

- Real people, diverse representation
- Modern Arab business settings
- Clean, professional environments
- Natural lighting

### Illustration Style

- Flat design with subtle gradients
- Consistent stroke weights
- Brand colors only
- Professional but approachable
