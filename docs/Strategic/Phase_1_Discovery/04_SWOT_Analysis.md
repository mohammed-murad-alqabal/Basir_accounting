## Strategic Implications

### Leverage Strengths + Opportunities (SO Strategy)

1. Use Arabic-first design to capture mandatory compliance market
2. Leverage dual-mode for unique positioning in underserved market
3. Apply AI to automate compliance, capture digital transformation wave

### Overcome Weaknesses via Opportunities (WO Strategy)

1. Use regulatory tailwind to build brand awareness
2. Leverage young population for viral growth despite small team
3. Use Open Banking to compensate for limited features initially

### Use Strengths to Address Threats (ST Strategy)

1. Use AI moat to differentiate from well-funded competitors
2. Use agile development to respond to regulatory changes
3. Use Arabic-first to counter global player improvements

### Minimize Weaknesses & Threats (WT Strategy)

1. Seek strategic funding to match competitors
2. Build security as core to prevent reputation damage
3. Focus on core market before spreading thin
