## Key Insights

### Market Characteristics

1. **Large Underserved Market**: $8.2B TAM with no Arabic-first dominant player
2. **Regulatory Tailwinds**: Mandatory e-invoicing driving adoption
3. **Realistic Target**: 10% SAM capture = $210M ARR
4. **Clear Path**: Bottom-up validation supports top-down estimates

### Strategic Implications

1. **Focus on SAM**: Prioritize 10 high-potential countries
2. **Start with SME**: Highest willingness to pay, clearest need
3. **Add Personal Later**: Large volume, lower ARPU
4. **Build for $1B**: Design infrastructure for scale
