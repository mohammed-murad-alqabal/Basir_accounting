## Persona 1: Khalid the Freelancer

### Profile

| Attribute      | Details                      |
| -------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Name**       | خالد (Khalid)                |
| **Age**        | 28-35                        |
| **Role**       | Freelance Designer/Developer |
| **Location**   | Riyadh, Saudi Arabia         |
| **Income**     | SAR 12,000-25,000/month      |
| **Tech Savvy** | High                         |
| **Mode**       | Business Basic               |

### Background

Khalid is a successful freelance UI/UX designer who left his corporate job 2 years ago. He works with 10-15 clients per month, mostly Saudi startups and SMEs. He uses his iPhone for most work tasks and values efficiency.

### Goals

- Create professional invoices quickly
- Track payments and chase overdue invoices
- Understand his monthly income and expenses
- Stay ZATCA compliant without hiring an accountant
- Appear professional to clients

### Pain Points

- "Creating invoices takes too long with current tools"
- "I forget which clients haven't paid"
- "ZATCA requirements confuse me"
- "Most apps feel too corporate or too basic"
- "I can't find good Arabic financial tools"

### Behaviors

- Creates 15-20 invoices per month
- Checks finances on mobile while commuting
- Prefers quick actions over detailed reports
- Values aesthetics and design quality
- Active on Twitter/X for business

### Quote

> "أريد تطبيق يجعلني أبدو محترفاً أمام عملائي بدون تعقيد"
> "I want an app that makes me look professional to clients without complexity"

### Basir Solution

- 30-second invoice creation
- Payment tracking with reminders
- ZATCA-compliant templates
- Clean, modern Arabic design
- Mobile-first experience

## Persona 2: Noura the Business Owner

### Profile

| Attribute      | Details                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Name**       | نورة (Noura)                    |
| **Age**        | 35-45                           |
| **Role**       | Small Business Owner (Boutique) |
| **Location**   | Jeddah, Saudi Arabia            |
| **Employees**  | 3-5                             |
| **Revenue**    | SAR 50,000-150,000/month        |
| **Tech Savvy** | Medium                          |
| **Mode**       | Business Pro                    |

### Background

Noura owns a successful women's fashion boutique with both physical and online presence. She manages inventory, employees, and customer relationships. She has an accountant who handles annual taxes but needs daily financial visibility.

### Goals

- Track daily sales and expenses
- Manage customer relationships
- Generate tax reports for accountant
- Understand profitability by product
- Separate business and personal finances

### Pain Points

- "I don't know if I'm actually profitable day-to-day"
- "My accountant only tells me things annually"
- "Managing inventory and invoices separately is chaos"
- "I want my team to help but worry about access"
- "Current software is too complicated"

### Behaviors

- Reviews finances every evening
- Delegates invoice creation to staff
- Needs multi-user access
- Values detailed reports
- Prefers WhatsApp for business communication

### Quote

> "أحتاج أعرف وضعي المالي الحقيقي، مو بس نهاية السنة"
> "I need to know my real financial situation, not just at year-end"

### Basir Solution

- Real-time financial dashboard
- Multi-user team access
- Inventory integration
- Tax-ready reports
- Customer CRM

## Persona 3: Ahmed the Individual

### Profile

| Attribute      | Details                 |
| -------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Name**       | أحمد (Ahmed)            |
| **Age**        | 25-32                   |
| **Role**       | Corporate Employee      |
| **Location**   | Dubai, UAE              |
| **Income**     | AED 15,000-30,000/month |
| **Tech Savvy** | High                    |
| **Mode**       | Personal Plus           |

### Background

Ahmed is a marketing manager at a tech company in Dubai. He earns a good salary but struggles to save money. He has multiple subscriptions, a car loan, and wants to buy an apartment soon. He uses fintech apps regularly.

### Goals

- Track where his money goes
- Build savings for apartment down payment
- Manage multiple subscriptions
- Set and achieve financial goals
- Understand spending patterns

### Pain Points

- "Money disappears and I don't know where"
- "I can't find Arabic budgeting apps"
- "Subscription tracking is messy"
- "I keep starting budgets but never stick to them"
- "I don't know how much I actually save"

### Behaviors

- Checks spending multiple times daily
- Uses Apple Pay for most purchases
- Likes gamification and progress tracking
- Shares goals with friends
- Prefers dark mode apps

### Quote

> "أبي أعرف وين راتبي يروح كل شهر"
> "I want to know where my salary goes each month"

### Basir Solution

- Automatic expense categorization
- Savings goals with progress tracking
- Subscription tracker
- Budget alerts and insights
- Arabic-first interface

| **Role**       | Tech Startup CEO |
| **Location**   | Cairo, Egypt     |
| **Employees**  | 10-25            |

- Delegates to finance manager
- Needs API integrations
- Values automation heavily
- Uses Slack, Notion, Google Workspace

### Quote

> "محتاجة نظام يكبر معانا من غير ما يكلفنا ثروة"
> "I need a system that grows with us without costing a fortune"

- Multi-entity management
- Custom reporting for investors
- Multi-country compliance
- API access for integrations
- Cash flow forecasting
