# Basir Accounting Standards (World-Class Excellence)

## 🩺 Financial Integrity & Precision

1.  **Immutability**: All financial transactions must be treated as immutable once finalized. Corrections must be handled via reversal and re-entry.
2.  **Precision**: Always use high-precision data types. Use `Decimal` (128-bit) for currency.
3.  **Double-Entry**: Every transaction must adhere to `Assets = Liabilities + Equity`.
4.  **IFRS 2025+ Compliance**:
    - **IFRS 18**: Early adoption of new presentation and disclosure standards (5-category IS).
    - **ISSB (S1/S2)**: Integration of sustainability-related financial disclosures.
5.  **Audit Readiness (SOX 404)**: Every record must be "audit-defensible" with a clear link to the underlying accounting standard and rationale.

## 📊 Logic & Recognition

1.  **5-Step Recognition**: Adhere to IFRS 15/ASC 606 for all revenue contracts.
2.  **Matching Principle**: Ensure automated expense accruals and revenue matching.
3.  **Hierarchical CoA**: CoA must support multi-entity mapping and IFRS 18 categories.

## ⚖️ Islamic Compliance (AAOIFI Alignment)

1.  **Purity Protocol**: Strict avoidance of involuntary Riba and Gharar in core logic.
2.  **Transparency**: Zero hidden fees or ambiguous contract terms.

## 🛠️ Implementation Rules

- **Immutable Ledger**: Use cryptographic hash-chaining for ledger entries.
- **Validation**: Rules-engine based validation of every transaction against IFRS/GAAP ontologies.
